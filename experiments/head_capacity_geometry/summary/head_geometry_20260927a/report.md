# Supervised development head geometry and R expressibility

These are five-fold held-out **supervised development** diagnostics, not TTA methods or final performance. ITW uses the unchanged 3,178 target10 IDs; WaveFake uses the fixed 4,096 paired development rows. H3 reproduces the prior supervised linear probe exactly (maximum held-out score difference 0 in both domains). H4 is the unchanged small nonlinear upper bound.

H0/H1 preserve Frozen AUC and EER **within every held-out fold** by construction. Their small pooled changes arise because each fold learns a different score calibration, which can reorder *between-fold* pairs. H2 improves AUC in all five folds of both domains, confirming that changing direction can improve ranking. However, H2 recovers only `39.98%` of H3's ITW AUC gain and `53.92%` of H3's WaveFake gain. Both are below the preregistered `80%` criterion; formal `DECISION_DIRECTION_SHIFT` (H2≈H3) is **not confirmed**. H2 holds source bias and head norm fixed, so this does not prove a free bias directly improves within-fold ranking; the constrained H2 training path may also limit its learned direction.

The H3 target head is nearly orthogonal to the source head in original 160D coordinates: mean source–target angle `83.37°` on ITW and `85.50°` on WaveFake. Unit-direction displacement norms are `1.330/1.358`. Raw fitted-bias changes average about `−9.94/+10.70`, respectively; these values depend on the fitted score scale and should not be interpreted as comparable thresholds by themselves. The two domains' mean unit-direction changes have cosine `0.664`, while 25 cross-fold pair cosines range `0.566–0.705`. This is descriptive geometry, not a transferable spoof direction: the prior direct ITW↔WaveFake supervised probe transfer underperformed destination Frozen in both directions.

The actual production adapter obeys `w_eff=w_s+U Rᵀ(Uᵀw_s)`. Float32 direct-adapter versus effective-head scores agree within `1.91e-6` across 20 random radius-0.1 R matrices and 32 real cached embeddings. `UᵀU` differs from identity by at most `2.53e-8`. Thus R can change the head only inside `span(U)`, with coefficient norm at most `0.1||Uᵀw_s||=0.048396`. After normalizing each supervised H3 head to the source head's norm, the R subspace explains only `17.26%` (ITW) and `17.14%` (WaveFake) of the supervised direction correction on average; the current radius can explain only `3.38%/3.31%` in squared-distance terms. **All five folds in both domains are below 0.5**, satisfying the predeclared `R_PARAMETERIZATION_MISMATCH` rule. These fractions characterize the particular supervised BCE head shifts and source U; they are not a universal bound on every task objective.

Decision: the fixed-classifier bounded R path is retired. Proceed to the predeclared label-free, source-anchored linear head prototype H-UA1. No target90 labels/metrics or final held-out metrics entered these diagnostics.

# Large-scale development confirmation — interpretation

This is a locked four-arm, six-order development confirmation. ITW uses the complete existing target10 (3,178), Codecfake uses the fixed 5,000-ID official-dev assignment, and ASVspoof2019 LA has 5,000 scored IDs with 4,972 covered by the official dev protocol. All 316,272 score rows and 4,968 local buffer records passed exact coverage, finite-value, fixed-order and reset checks before selected labels opened. Frozen and Per-sample Base were independently rerun in every order and were exactly invariant by ID. The first post-score analysis stopped on LA protocol coverage; its failure record remains, and the documented 4,972-ID official-protocol metric subset was used without redrawing or rescoring.

Codecfake's old 512 result (`+0.009940/+0.012231` AUC for B32 Base/O1 in its original fixed order) **does not replicate** on the 5,000-ID set across six orders. The large-set mean effects are below the preregistered `+0.005` practical floor and their effect-to-order-variability ratios are only `0.335/0.373` versus the required `2`. Each arm is positive in only three of six orders; paired ΔAUC bootstrap intervals have positive lower bounds in only two orders. The manifest order gives `+0.004826/+0.006582`, while several shuffled orders are negative. On the old 512 IDs nested inside the new buffer context, six-order mean effects are `-0.000081/+0.000306` (Base/O1); the additional 4,488 IDs give `+0.000757/+0.001097`. The fixed-order result is therefore highly sensitive to which other samples share an R.

ITW's absolute local effect is at most a few `1e-5`, with local paired ΔAUC intervals generally spanning zero. It is **not practically meaningful**, even if a larger sample could narrow an interval. Per-sample Base has a small `+0.000557` AUC movement but EER worsens by `+0.000986`, and that arm was not the local-context hypothesis. ASVspoof2019 LA is already near-perfect under Frozen; its local AUC changes are tiny and negative, so it provides no positive replication and has little headroom as a source-related official-dev domain.

**Composition limitation:** In Codecfake, sample rate and class are perfectly confounded in the locked 5,000: all 2,140 samples at 16 kHz and all 2,174 at 24 kHz are spoof; all 460 at 44.1 kHz and all 226 at 48 kHz are bonafide. No sample-rate stratum has both classes, so a within-rate spoof-ranking check is impossible. The earlier fixed 512 has the same rate/class separation (431 spoof at 16/24 kHz; 81 bonafide at 44.1/48 kHz). Codecfake AUC is therefore not separable from source-format and compatibility effects in this resource. The spoof attack IDs are broadly represented among the 4,314 spoof files, but their representation cannot remove the sample-rate confound. This finding came from a post-score composition audit and was not used to alter samples or updates.

**Preregistered decision: `SMALL_DEVELOPMENT_ARTIFACT`.** There is no stable effect of practical size in two independent two-class development domains; Codecfake's larger-set mean effect is small and order variability dominates it. Local context is not confirmed as a spoof-discriminative mechanism. Promote no candidate and do not add a new TTA module, expand B, tune K/lr/rho, or open target90/final held-out metrics. Bootstrap intervals and six-order variation are development diagnostics, not final claims. Local-O1 versus Per-sample O1 is not estimable under the strict four-arm roster; the valid local-versus-per-sample comparison is against Per-sample Base.
